Mada Plus – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Sinetra Plus is a browser extension that helps ISP technicians work faster in the CRM and on customer routers. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

Summary

Mada Plus does not collect, sell or share your data. Everything it handles stays on your device. The extension has no server and sends nothing to the developer or to any third party.

Data the extension handles

  • Settings: which features are switched on, and the router open mode (popup or new tab). Stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local).
  • Saved router logins: usernames and passwords that you type into the extension popup, used only to fill router login forms. Stored locally in your browser and never transmitted.
  • Page content: the extension reads text on the CRM and router pages you visit (for example the router IP address, customer mobile number, router WAN username, LAN/DHCP, port forwarding and DMZ settings) only to show the helper buttons and to perform the actions you request. This data is processed in your browser and is not sent anywhere.
  • Exported files: when you press "Save JSON", router settings are written to a JSON file that is downloaded to your own computer. You decide where it is kept. "Load JSON" reads a file that you choose and applies it to the router you have open.
  • Root Splitter list: a temporary copy of the Root Splitter names and IDs fetched from the company CRM, cached locally to speed up searches.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for any purpose other than its single purpose described above.
  • It does not use data for creditworthiness or lending decisions.
  • It does not track browsing history, clicks or keystrokes.
  • It does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

  • tabs: to open the router page, and the search pages, in a popup window or new tab.
  • storage: to keep your settings and saved logins on your device.
  • Access to http/https sites: customer routers are reached by IP address, which differs for every customer, so the helper must be able to run on those pages and on the CRM.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes all data it stored. You can also delete saved logins at any time from the extension popup.
